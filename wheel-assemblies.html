What You Receive

One part number. Zero shop time.

Every Toota wheel assembly ships as a finished, serialized unit. Receive it, bolt it to the hub, torque the lugs, and the vehicle rolls.

  • Single or two-piece wheels — matched to your platform and drawing package.
  • Composite RunFlat installed — with high-temp synthetic lubricant applied.
  • Tire mounted and inflated — beads seated, pressures set.
  • Torqued to specification — every fastener verified and documented.
  • Optional CARC paint — Chemical Agent Resistant Coating for tactical finishes.
  • Kitted and palletized — vendor kits for land vehicle programs, labeled to your contract.

Spec
