Core Competencies

What we're built to deliver.

Wheel Assembly Manufacturing

Single and two-piece wheel assemblies built to drawing packages — RunFlat installed, tire mounted, torqued, documented, and optionally CARC-painted.

Composite RunFlat Supply

Segmented antiballistic composite RunFlat systems and high-temperature synthetic runflat lubricant, sized to platform.

Tire Distribution & Kitting

General and Continental truck, trailer, and specialty tires — supplied loose or as labeled vendor kits for land vehicle programs.

Program Support

Fast quote turnaround on solicitations and NSNs, schedule-driven delivery, and direct access to the people doing the work.

Differentiators

Why buyers pick Toota.

  • Small business agility — decisions and quotes in days, not quarters, and set-aside eligibility for your goals.
  • Next-generation runflat technology — segmented composite systems that install with hand tools — no hydraulic press or depot equipment required.
  • Vertical scope — insert, lubricant, tire, and finished assembly from one CAGE code, cutting your vendor count and integration risk.
  • In-house assembly — built and verified at our New Jersey facility, not brokered.
