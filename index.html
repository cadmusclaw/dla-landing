The Composite Advantage

Engineered to outperform.

Segmented composite technology moves the numbers that matter to a fleet — weight, install time, field serviceability, and lifecycle cost. Here's how it compares to a traditional rubber runflat.

Segmented Composite RunFlat Traditional Rubber RunFlat
Installation ✓ Three bolt-together segments — installed with hand tools by your own crew Requires hydraulic press or dedicated fitting machine
Weight ✓ Composite construction — typically ~15% lighter, saving fuel and payload Solid rubber adds more unsprung weight per wheel
Field serviceability ✓ Segments can be fitted or swapped forward-deployed, no depot trip Wheel typically shipped to equipped facility for service
Protection ✓ Antiballistic composite engineered for the armored vehicle market Rubber insert supports the tire at a higher weight per wheel
Mobility after deflation ✓ Up to 50 km of continued driving after ballistic or terrain damage Comparable distances at a higher weight per wheel
