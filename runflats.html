How It Works

A composite ring that carries the load when the air is gone.

Three antiballistic composite segments bolt around the wheel rim inside the tire. Under normal driving they're along for the ride. The moment the tire loses pressure — from ballistic damage, terrain, or sabotage — the RunFlat becomes the load path, keeping the tire on the bead and the vehicle under the driver's control.

  • Keeps the driver in control after ballistic or terrain-related tire damage.
  • Holds the bead so the deflated tire stays seated at speed and under maneuver.
  • Composite, not rubber — less unsprung mass at every corner of the vehicle.
  • Field-serviceable — unbolts for inspection or replacement without a depot.
Why Segmented Composite

Why segmented composite.

Segmented composite construction delivers advantages a solid rubber insert can't — at every corner of the vehicle.

Lighter Corner Weight

Roughly 15% lighter than rubber equivalents. Multiply that across every wheel position and you get back payload, fuel economy, and suspension life.

Faster to Field

Bolt-together segments install with hand tools. No hydraulic press, no fitting machine, no shipping wheels to a depot and waiting.

Built for Armor

Antiballistic composite designed for the armored and security vehicle market — protection engineered in, not just mass added on.

Serviceable, Reusable

Unbolt to inspect, service, or transfer between wheels. A segmented system is an asset you maintain, not a consumable you press in once.

RunFlat Lubricant

The unsung hero of run-flat range.

When a tire runs deflated, it drags against the insert and generates serious heat. Our high-temperature synthetic RunFlat lubricant goes in at installation and slashes that friction — protecting the tire, protecting the insert, and extending how far you can actually drive on a flat.

  • High-temperature synthetic formulation — stable under sustained run-flat heat.
  • Applied once, at install — no in-service maintenance.
  • Protects both components — tire carcass and composite insert.
  • Available with every RunFlat order — or supplied separately for your existing fleet program.

Questions

RunFlat FAQ

How far can a vehicle drive after losing tire pressure?

With the system fitted and lubricated, up to 50 km of continued mobility is achievable following ballistic or terrain-related deflation — enough to leave the threat area and reach support, not just limp to the shoulder.

Do we need special equipment to install these?

No. That's the point. The three composite segments bolt together around the rim with standard hand tools and a torque wrench. See the interactive installation walkthrough.

How much weight does composite save over rubber?

Typically around 15% versus a comparable rubber runflat — and that's rotating, unsprung mass, where weight savings pay off most in handling, fuel, and component life.

Is the lubricant required?

Strongly recommended. Run-flat operation generates friction heat between tire and insert; the high-temperature synthetic lubricant is what keeps that heat from destroying the tire early and cutting your run-flat range short.

Can Toota supply the complete wheel — not just the insert?

Yes. We build complete single and two-piece wheel assemblies — rim, RunFlat, lubricant, and tire — mounted, torqued, optionally CARC-painted, and delivered ready to bolt on.
